# Profile & General Mobile Test Cases

## TC-PRO-001 — Open account/profile
**Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Functional  
**Steps:** Open Account/Profile while signed in.  
**Expected:** Correct account area loads.

## TC-PRO-002 — Open settings
**Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Functional  
**Steps:** Open Account then Settings.  
**Expected:** Available settings load and controls work.

## TC-PRO-003 — Change location permission in OS
**Priority:** High | **Type:** Recovery  
**Steps:** Change location permission in device settings, return to app, try location feature.  
**Expected:** App responds correctly to new permission state.

## TC-PRO-004 — Large text
**Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Accessibility/Usability  
**Steps:** Increase system text/display size and inspect major screens.  
**Expected:** Important content remains understandable/usable without severe overlap.

## TC-PRO-005 — Relaunch
**Priority:** High | **Type:** Stability  
**Steps:** Close and relaunch app.  
**Expected:** App launches without crash and restores appropriate state.

## TC-PRO-006 — Wi-Fi/mobile-data switch
**Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Network  
**Steps:** Switch networks during safe non-destructive navigation.  
**Expected:** App stays stable and reconnects appropriately.

## TC-PRO-007 — Rapid navigation
**Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Exploratory  
**Steps:** Move repeatedly among major navigation areas.  
**Expected:** No crash, freeze, duplicate screen, or corrupted navigation.

## TC-PRO-008 — Sensitive-data display
**Priority:** High | **Type:** Privacy/UI  
**Steps:** Inspect authorized account/trip/payment screens.  
**Expected:** Sensitive values are not unnecessarily exposed.
